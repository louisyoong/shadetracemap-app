ShadeTrace Map

User Privacy Choices

Effective July 19, 2026

ShadeTrace Map has no user accounts, targeted advertising, analytics, or developer-operated database of user searches. The developer does not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

Delete local app data

ShadeTrace Map stores limited preferences on your device. To erase them completely on iPhone or iPad:

  1. Touch and hold the ShadeTrace Map icon.
  2. Select Remove App.
  3. Select Delete App and confirm.

Reinstalling the app creates a fresh local installation.

Control network requests

Maps, place search, and weather require connections to third-party services. You can avoid sending a place search or selected coordinates to those providers by not using those online features. You may also restrict the app’s network access through device or network controls where available.

Access, correction, and deletion requests

Because the developer does not maintain user accounts or a server-side history of searches and selected locations, the developer generally has no user profile to access, correct, or delete.

Requests handled directly by OpenStreetMap Nominatim, Open-Meteo, OpenFreeMap, or Esri are subject to each provider’s privacy process. Links to their policies are listed in the Privacy Policy.

Contact the developer

If you have a privacy question or believe information was submitted through a support interaction, contact the developer through the support tracker. Do not include passwords, precise locations, or other sensitive information in a public issue.
